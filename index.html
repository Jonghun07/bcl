About BCL

Building the Cognitive Brain of Future Buildings

BCL aims to establish Building Cognition as a new interdisciplinary research paradigm that bridges architecture, building engineering, artificial intelligence, and digital-twin technologies.

Building Cognition Lab develops the scientific foundation for next-generation intelligent buildings.

Our research integrates Building Information Modeling (BIM), Digital Twins, lightweight AI models for edge devices, building energy and environmental knowledge graphs, and physics-based simulation to enable buildings to perceive, understand, reason, predict, learn, and autonomously make decisions.

The framework is inspired by neuroscience. Just as the brain integrates signals from its sensory organs, forms memory, and reasons before acting, BCL treats a building as a cognitive system: in-situ sensors and building data streams (BEMS & BAS data, operational logs and metadata, U-value, airtightness, IAQ, thermal comfort, occupant productivity) serve as its senses, digital twins and knowledge graphs form its memory and world-model, and edge AI provides the reasoning that turns perception into action — mirrored in our logo, where a neuron receives inputs, decides whether to fire, and propagates an output.

This cognitive-neuroscience perspective is grounded in a two-decade empirical record of field measurement and validated building-physics models, ensuring that every layer of intelligence is anchored in real, measured performance rather than assumption.

Our vision is to create buildings that continuously understand themselves and their surrounding environments, optimize their own performance, and operate autonomously.

THE MARK

Our logo is built around a single artificial neuron — the fundamental unit of intelligence.

Signals flow from the two nodes on the left into the central neuron, where information is integrated and a decision is made.
The resulting action propagates to the nodes on the right, representing the cognitive cycle of perceive, reason, and act.

This simple neural structure reflects our vision of Building Cognition: transforming buildings from passive physical systems into intelligent agents capable of understanding their environment, making autonomous decisions, and continuously improving their performance.

The surrounding indigo-to-cyan gradient symbolizes the architectural space in which people live and work. Its cool blue tones represent healthy, comfortable, and sustainable indoor environments — the ultimate purpose of building intelligence.

Platform

BIM2Everything

BIM2Everything(B2E) is the digital research platform for developing, validating, and deploying Building Cognition.

Purpose

Turn a single BIM/IFC model into every building-performance analysis — one geometry, every domain — so evidence flows automatically from design to operation.

Key Capabilities
  • One-pass extraction of geometry, metadata, and semantics from BIM/IFC
  • Auto-generated digital twins linked to live BEMS/BAS & operational data
  • Physics-based simulation across energy, environment, and safety
  • Building energy & environmental knowledge graphs for reasoning
  • AI-agent-driven analysis, diagnostics, and automated reporting

Integrated Building Cognition Pipeline

A unified workflow connects BIM, Digital Twins, physics-based simulation, semantic knowledge, and AI reasoning.
IFC→ Data Processing→ Digital Twin→ Simulation→ Knowledge Graph→ AI Agent→ Report

Dr. Jonghun Kim is the founding director of the Building Cognition Lab and Professor in the Department of Architectural Engineering at Dong-A University. He previously served as Professor at the University of Science & Technology (UST, 2021–2026) and as Principal Researcher and Head of the Building Energy Group at the Korea Institute of Energy Research (KIER, 2012–2026).

As a Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship student, he studied at the University of Tokyo, where he earned his Ph.D. in Architectural Environment Engineering (energy & indoor air quality) in 2011.

His research bridges building physics, indoor environmental quality, and artificial intelligence — advancing digital twins, building knowledge graphs, and physics-informed AI toward fully autonomous buildings. He holds international credentials including Certified Passive House Designer and Certified Measurement & Verification Professional.

In recognition of his contributions to the nation's building energy and carbon-neutrality agenda, he has been honored with four Ministerial Commendations from the Korean government — awarded by the Ministers of Land, Infrastructure and Transport, Science and ICT, and Trade, Industry and Energy — among numerous other national and academic awards.
